▼Visualizer
Auto-cycle modes

Musical colour hands the hue over to the track's dominant pitch class, so the palette moves with the harmony rather than the clock.

▼Spectrum Layers

Each mode's signature motion sits on one band; these layers add the rest of the spectrum around it. Toggle with keys 1–4.

Adaptive range gives every band its own rolling floor and ceiling, so quiet parts of the spectrum stay expressive instead of sitting at zero. Drop it toward 0% for raw, unequalised levels.

▼Interaction

Every mode responds to the pointer. Move to paint or bend the field, hold to pull it toward you, shift-hold or right-hold to push it away.

Every mode responds to touch. Drag to paint or bend the field, hold to pull it in, two fingers to push away.

▼Fluid Physics

Fractal fold mirrors and rescales the dye field over and over, so the fluid picks up self-similar detail that keeps resolving as it moves. auto uses each mode's own setting.

▼Fractal Engine

Detail sets the iteration budget — higher means finer filigree and more GPU load. The escape-time modes zoom forever on their own; the pointer steers where they go.

iPhone Safari has no fullscreen API. For an edge-to-edge, chrome-free visualizer, use Share → Add to Home Screen and launch it from the icon.

Keyboard: H panel · F fullscreen · R random · C clear · 1–4 layers · Space play/pause · ← → change mode

Touch: tap the edge handle to slide the panel away, or swipe left / right anywhere on the visualizer. Swipe up / down to change mode.
